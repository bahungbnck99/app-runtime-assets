Sensitive-content data provenance decision
==========================================

Status: BLOCKED FOR PRODUCTION RELEASE

The candidate image, anime-tagging and temporal model cards do not provide a complete,
auditable inventory of training data rights for the target commercial use.
The repository also does not contain a rights-cleared cross-domain
qualification set covering live action, animation/game, medical, news and
sports video.

The pack builder may produce a local candidate archive for engineering
tests. It must not publish manifests/registry entries until a qualification
report records:

- a rights-cleared model and dataset decision;
- per-domain segment precision/recall and false-positive/false-negative data;
- calibrated sensitive, balanced and low-false-positive profiles;
- CPU qualification and DirectML GPU qualification, including automatic CPU fallback;
- long-video, cancellation, corruption and memory/VRAM soak results.
